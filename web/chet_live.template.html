muEDM Phase I · PSI · Cylindrical Helix Tracker

Every decay positron, fitted live

A Michel positron from a muon on the 30 mm frozen-spin orbit curls through four stereo-fibre cylinders in a 3 T field. Each second this page simulates one decay, builds space points from u/v fibre coincidences, seeds, assigns hits and fits the helix. The reconstruction runs in your browser, with the same algorithm as the chet-tracking package.
